E-commerce Sales Analysis
Analyzed e-commerce sales data to understand customer behavior, product performance, and overall business trends. The project focuses on extracting meaningful insights through data analysis and visualization to support better business decisions.

Tech Used:

Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

What I Did:

Cleaned and processed the dataset

Performed exploratory data analysis (EDA)

Analyzed sales trends, customer preferences, and product performance

Created visualizations and business insights using charts and graphs

Identified key factors affecting sales and revenue

Outcome:
Generated actionable insights from e-commerce data that can help improve sales strategies, customer understanding, and overall business performance.
